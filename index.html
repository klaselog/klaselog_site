How it fits your school

It works the way your school already works.

One login, not several

Grades, attendance, parent updates and report cards sit behind a single sign-in, in place of the daily shuffle between the portal, Teams, and spreadsheets.

Configured to your grading system

Grading periods and weightings, written 40 · performance 40 · quarterly 20, set per school, aligned to the revised DepEd system with its 75-and-up descriptors.

Role-based access for everyone

Separate portals for admins, teachers, students, and parents. Coordinators and assistant principals cross-check gradebooks; parent access across all levels.

Hands-on onboarding

We set KlaseLog up with your staff and train teachers directly, including those who aren't comfortable with technology. Ease of use is the first priority.

Data protection

Your students' records, guarded like the school's own files.

For a school, nothing matters more than keeping family data safe. KlaseLog is built around that from the first line of code, not bolted on afterward.

Strict role-based access

Every record sits behind permissions. Teachers, coordinators, admins and parents each see only what they should, never more.

The school owns its data

Your records stay yours, exportable anytime. Never sold, never shared, and never used to train anything.

Protected against loss & leaks

Encrypted storage and offline-safe records, with safeguards built specifically to prevent leaks and data loss.

Philippine Data Privacy Act

Designed around RA 10173 and reviewed with privacy counsel, compliance your board can stand behind.

About us

Built from inside the classroom, not a boardroom.

KlaseLog started with a simple observation in Cebu: the schools working hardest to keep parents informed were buried in the most manual work. One of us lived that system as a student; the other builds the technology. Together we're making grade transparency something a school can switch on, without the overhead.
